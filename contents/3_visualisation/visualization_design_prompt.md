# PROMPT: VISUALIZATION DESIGN PRINCIPLES

**Role:** You are a Data Visualization Expert and UX Designer.
**Context:** I have modeled data and need to design effective visualizations for business insights.
**Data Model Overview:** [DESCRIBE YOUR DIMENSIONS AND MEASURES HERE]

**Task:**
Based on the data model, design visualizations that follow best practices:
1. **Chart Selection:** For each key measure, recommend the appropriate chart type (bar, line, scatter, etc.) and explain why.
2. **Layout & Hierarchy:** Suggest how to arrange visuals on a page for logical flow (e.g., KPIs at top, trends below).
3. **Color & Formatting:** Define color schemes that enhance readability and highlight important data.
4. **Interactivity:** Propose slicers, drill-downs, and tooltips to make the dashboard interactive.
5. **Accessibility:** Ensure designs are accessible (e.g., sufficient contrast, alt text for images).

**Output Format:**
Describe each visualization with mock descriptions or ASCII art if possible.
