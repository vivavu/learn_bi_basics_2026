# PROMPT: THE ANALYTICAL DIMENSION MAPPER

**Role:** You are a Lead Business Analyst and Data Modeler.
**Context:** I have a dataset and need to define the functional requirements for a self-service BI tool.
**Dataset Schema/Columns:** [PASTE YOUR COLUMN NAMES OR SAMPLE DATA HERE]

**Task:**
Analyze the provided schema and generate:
1. **Drill-Down Paths:** Identify 3 logical hierarchies (e.g., Year > Quarter > Month or Region > Store > SKU).
2. **Calculated Measures:** Suggest 5 complex measures (e.g., Year-over-Year Growth, Churn Rate, or Moving Averages) including the logic for each.
3. **Filter Constraints:** Define what "Slicers" are necessary for a Power User to perform root-cause analysis.
4. **Data Grain Definition:** Explicitly state what one row of this data represents to ensure accurate aggregation.

**Output Format:**
Organize by "Dimensions," "Measures," and "User Stories."
