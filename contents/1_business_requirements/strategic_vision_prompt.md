# PROMPT: THE EXECUTIVE STRATEGY ALIGNMENT

**Role:** You are a Senior BI Strategy Consultant.
**Context:** I am starting a new data project and need to define high-level business requirements that align with executive goals.
**Input Data Description:** [DESCRIBE YOUR DATASET HERE - e.g., "5 years of global retail sales, inventory levels, and shipping logs"]

**Task:**
Based on the dataset described, please generate:
1. **Three Primary KPIs:** Define the most critical North Star metrics this data should track for a VP-level stakeholder.
2. **The "So What" Scenarios:** Describe 3 specific business decisions an executive could make if they saw a 10% variance in these metrics.
3. **Strategic Questions:** List 5 questions this dataset *must* answer to prove its ROI to the board.

**Output Format:**
Provide the response in a structured table for the KPIs and bullet points for the scenarios.
